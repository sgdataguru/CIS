# Sentinel project context

Sentinel is a device-and-platform venture for security agencies and their property-owner clients in Singapore. It is designed to make security-officer service delivery verifiable through a managed wearable, a secure cloud platform, and a control-room experience.

## Business model and users

- **Primary buyer:** property owners and managers of condominiums, commercial buildings, and industrial sites.
- **Operational users:** security officers, control-room operators, site supervisors, and tenant/client administrators.
- **Commercial model:** subscription service; wearable devices can be recycled between officers.
- **Go-to-market:** client-mandated assurance, targeting the top 25% of agencies able to absorb device costs.

## MVP boundaries

The MVP does not replace site SOP systems or provide a general-purpose assistant. It delivers short, critical emergency prompts, on-demand retrieval from approved security SOPs, and incident-report drafts. Personal calls and non-work/off-topic queries are blocked.

## Constraints

- Wrist-worn vendor device; target unit cost SGD 20–30, hard ceiling SGD 100.
- Cellular SIM only; Wi-Fi cannot be assumed at sites.
- Built-in GPS to detect site departure and prevent shift handover evasion.
- Basic camera only; it is not intended for high-spec contextual video interpretation.
- Device sourcing/prototyping in India or Shenzhen with a 3–4 year supplier continuity commitment.
- Three-to-four month rollout ambition; device selection and unit economics are the critical path.
