# Vendor wearable MVP baseline

- Wrist-worn wearable form factor.
- LTE/4G cellular SIM connectivity; do not depend on Wi-Fi.
- Integrated GPS for location and site-departure policy events.
- Basic camera and voice interface; no high-spec video analytics requirement.
- Minimum 24-hour battery-life target under representative use.
- Secure device identity, remote provisioning, firmware version reporting, and OTA update support.
- Supplier must demonstrate 3–4 years of production continuity, secure supply-chain practices, and viable India or Shenzhen manufacturing.

All specifications remain subject to supplier diligence, pilot testing, certification, and total-cost validation.
